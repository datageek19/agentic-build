use case 1: AI-Powered Contract Lifecycle Management
Design, develop, and validate an agentic Contract Lifecycle Management solution that integrates with SAP Ariba (not part of PoC) and supports contract review, deviation detection, risk assessment, clause analysis, and redlining recommendations. The PoC will demonstrate measurable reduction in manual contract review effort, faster turnaround times, and a scalable foundation for future contract governance capabilities

Use Case 1 Objectives – Contract Lifecycle Management
-	Integrate with existing S2P systems (e.g. SAP Ariba) to facilitate an intuitive end-to-end experience for multiple personas (end user/requester, procurement agent, functional stakeholder, supplier) -> not part of PoC
-	Automate contract analysis and review activities, including clause extraction, deviation detection, policy compliance checks, risk identification, and redlining recommendations.
-	Accelerate contract turnaround times by enabling AI-assisted review workflows that help stakeholders identify issues, prioritize risks, and resolve contract exceptions more efficiently; note multiple stakeholders are typically involved and provide reviews and input concurrently.
-	Improve contract quality and governance through standardized contract evaluations, automated risk scoring, and enhanced visibility into contractual deviations and compliance requirements.
-	Demonstrate the effectiveness of agentic AI capabilities in orchestrating contract review tasks, generating recommendations, and supporting decision-making while maintaining human oversight.
-	Establish a scalable foundation on GCP that can support future expansion of AI-enabled contract lifecycle management capabilities (e.g. analytics and insights) across additional categories, business units, and enterprise contract governance processes.
